## Found while capturing

1. **028 RUN symbol** is tagged "Citadines Xingqing Palace Xi'an", which is a hotel. Change it to the Terracotta Army listing, or Lintong or Xi'an.
2. **026 INVENTORY symbol** repeats the full reel caption, including the YouTube-style title line, instead of the short symbol caption.
3. **021 FAITH symbol** uses the same line as its reel ("The receiver precedes the signal.").
4. **025 PARALLAX symbol** and the 020–021 symbols have no location tag. 022 and 023 are tagged "Worldwide".
5. **Gaps in NVS:** 015 and 017 THRESHOLD were never posted on Instagram (015A WARMUP was). Covers for 017 exist in the repo.
6. **Reel title formats vary:** "027| BEARING", "026 | INVENTORY", "NDCH 023 | AXIOM", and "NVS_0NN:" or "NVS_0NN |" on older reels. The current standard is "NNN | TITLE".
7. **First comments** were found only on 024 FRAMES, 023 AXIOM, 022 THE STATUE, 028 RUN and FREQ 001.
8. **Reel pages don't show a location**, so a reel's tag couldn't be confirmed from Instagram. Symbols carry the visible tags.

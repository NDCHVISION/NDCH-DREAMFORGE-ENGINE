## **A. Party anthems (hook-repeat, 120–130 BPM)**

**A1. Heavy-bass party (base template)**



Heavy-bass party anthem, 128 BPM, 4/4. Deep Black male baritone,
playful, confident, clear and upfront. Punchy kick, huge clean sub,
sliding 808, crisp claps, bright synth stabs, short brass hits, crowd
shouts. Seven repetitions per hook line. No narrative verses. Same
catchy melody for every hook line; lead louder than crowd. Whisper
break, rising claps, one-beat silence, giant clean bass drop. Fun,
danceable, loopable.

**A2. Afrobeats-techno with highlife inspiration**



Afrobeats-techno party anthem with Ghanaian highlife inspiration, 124
BPM, 4/4. Deep Black male baritone, playful, confident, clear and
upfront. Driving four-on-the-floor kick, rolling afro-percussion,
shaker, bell, syncopated clap, clean sub-bass, sliding 808 accents,
bright interlocking highlife guitars, warm synth stabs. Original melody
and riffs inspired by highlife warmth; no copied songs. Seven
repetitions per hook line. No narrative verses. Lead louder than crowd.
Soft women's harmonies answer the lead. Whisper break with guitar and
shaker, rising percussion, one-beat silence, giant clean bass drop.
Danceable, loopable.

**A3. Arabesque electronic party**



Arabesque electronic party anthem, 124 BPM, 4/4. Deep Black male
baritone, playful, confident, clear and upfront. Hijaz-style melodic
scale, oud and qanun riffs, darbuka and tambourine groove,

## **B. Masculine / assertive (dark, 70–130 BPM)**

**B1. Hip-hop and techno hybrid (assertive)**



Modern hip-hop and techno hybrid, 130 BPM, 4/4. Deep, gritty Black male
baritone: commanding, assertive, magnetic swagger, never screaming.
Massive sliding 808s, distorted upper bass over clean sub, punchy kick,
tight claps, rolling hats, dark synth stabs, driving techno pulse.
Exactly seven repetitions of the hook phrase per main hook. Same melodic
contour every time; lead louder than crowd. Low gang-vocal responses,
chest resonance. Whisper break with bass alone, rising percussion,
one-beat silence, giant 808 drop. Clear upfront vocals, no narrative
verses. Evolve the bass patterns, drum dropouts, filter movement, and
octave doubles.

**B2. Dark trap, whispered (about 200 characters)**



Dark trap, heavy 808 sub bass, distorted bass drops, slow hypnotic 70
BPM, minimal piano stabs, deep whispered male vocal, chant-like
repetition, cinematic tension, sparse hi-hats, confident menacing mood

**B3. Dark trap, long form**



Clean sub with distorted upper bass, dry kick, sparse hats, isolated
minor-key piano stabs. Deep male baritone in hushed, close-mic whispers,
roomy and unprocessed, calm and controlled, with low-octave doubles and
the same melodic contour for both hook phrases. Clear upfront vocals,
one-beat silences before drops, final bass drop hits through stark
contrast.

## **E. Modular building blocks**

Swap these into any style above.

- **Hook rule:** Seven repetitions per hook line. Same melodic contour
  > every time. Lead louder than crowd.

- **Dynamics:** Whisper break, rising percussion, one-beat silence,
  > giant clean bass drop.

- **Vocal tone options:** Deep Black male baritone, playful /
  > commanding, never screaming / hushed close-mic whisper / smooth,
  > smiling R&B

- **Loop ending:** Seamless loop; final note resolves into the first
  > note.

- **Safety line:** Original riffs; no copied songs.

To mix styles, use one family (A–D) as the base and add one or two E
blocks. The session used that pattern for most of the tracks.

# Symbol prompt (reel critique + preview poster)

The prompt NDCH uses in ChatGPT to critique a finished reel and design its symbol: the preview poster that sits beside the reel on the Instagram grid. Saved 2026-11-25 as written. Suggested additions follow it.

## Prompt (as used)

```text
Review the attached reel, NDCH_REEL_[NNN]_[TITLE], and give me your honest creative critique in two parts.

PART 1: The reel
- Overall impression: concept, pacing, mood, and how clearly it communicates "[TITLE]."
- Strongest moments and weakest moments, with timestamps where possible.
- Visual consistency with the earlier NDCH reels in this project: palette, lighting, motion language, and symbolic tone.
- Any edits you would make (cuts, timing, sound, text) to strengthen it.

PART 2: The preview poster (SYMBOL)
Design direction for a poster in the same series as the previous NDCH preview posters.

The rule for the SYMBOL: it must relate to the reel's theme without literally depicting or explaining it. It should be a creative iteration, meaning an abstract, suggestive visual translation of the idea. It should not be an illustration of what happens in the reel.

Please give me:
1. 3 to 5 distinct symbol concepts for "[TITLE]." For each one, explain what it evokes and why it relates without telling.
2. Your single recommended concept and why it is strongest.
3. Composition guidance for the recommended concept: focal point, symbol scale and placement, negative space, depth and layering, lighting, color palette, and typography placement. Keep all of it consistent with the earlier posters.
4. A final image-generation prompt for the recommended poster, ready to paste.

Constraints:
- Do not make the symbol literal. Avoid anything that directly shows the reel's content.
- Keep the series identity: same framing logic, same level of restraint, same premium cinematic tone.
- Flag anything in the reel or concept that feels too obvious, too busy, or off-series, and say how to fix it.

Be direct and specific. Prioritize critique over praise.
```

## Suggested additions

These cover what the process already expects but the prompt doesn't yet say. Paste them under Constraints.

```text
- The NDCH emblem (the arch-and-Y mark) must appear in the symbol, integrated into the scene as an object, inlay or light, never as a flat logo stamp.
- No text, numbers or lettering anywhere in the symbol image.
- Output format: 1080 × 1440 (3:4), so the Instagram grid shows the whole image.
- Palette: black stone and warm antique gold, with one restrained accent at most. No new colors without a reason tied to the theme.
- Before giving the final prompt, grade the recommended concept 1–10 on each of: not literal, series fidelity, emblem integration, restraint, readable at thumbnail size. Revise until every score is 8 or higher, then show the scores.
- Also give the grid caption in this exact format:
  NNN | TITLE
  (blank line)
  two short lines that echo the theme without explaining it
  #NDCH plus up to 2 hashtags
```

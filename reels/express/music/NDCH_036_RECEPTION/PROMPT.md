# NDCH 036 RECEPTION: Suno prompt

Paste into Suno (v5, **Instrumental on**). Generate 2-4 takes and add the MP3s to
[`candidates/`](candidates/). The next render scores every take against the narration and
keeps the best 45 s window; `music.json` and `chosen.mp3` here record the result.

**Title**

```
RECEPTION - The Open Door
```

**Style of Music**

```
warm hopeful cinematic instrumental, 72 BPM, E major, gentle fingerpicked nylon-string guitar and soft upright piano in short patient phrases, like someone waiting at a door, warm solo cello enters around 0:17, slow steady build with soft violins underneath, no drums, a bright open swell that lifts around 0:38 like sunlight pouring through a doorway, then settles warm and calm, intimate and tender, starts and ends on the same warm chord for a seamless loop
```

**Exclude styles**

```
vocals, choir, drums, percussion, trap, EDM, lo-fi beat, dark, ominous, horror, drone, braams, risers, sub-bass hits, sad, minor key, dorian, felt piano close-mic
```

Why this bed: the reel turns from a boy knocking at a closed door to a man walking into a warm,
sunlit room. The music follows that arc: patient and small in the hook, a cello at the 17 s
pivot (the boy becomes a teenager), a bright swell at 38 s when the door is wide open. Guitar
and upright piano in E major keep it apart from 035 PRESENCE (felt piano, D dorian), so the
two reels never share a sound.

# Instagram playbook (@ndchvision)

These rules come from the account's own numbers (`posts/instagram/baseline_*.json`, first captured 2026-11-25). Re-capture monthly and update this file when the numbers say something new.

## Where we are (30 days to Nov 24)

- **Views:** 376 views from 252 viewers. 71% were non-followers, so Instagram is already showing the work to new people.
- **What gets seen:** reels carry 84% of views, stories 11% and posts 6%.
- **Reach is hit-or-miss:** FRAMES (121) and BEARING (117) broke out. Every other reel stopped at 10–20.
- **Conversion is the gap:** those 252 viewers made **2 profile visits** and 4 accounts engaged. People watch, then leave, because nothing tells them what NDCH is or why they should follow.

## House style (keep)

- Every reel has a matching symbol still on the grid: the same number, the same landmark location, and a short caption (`NNN | TITLE`, two lines, `#NDCH` plus up to 2 tags). The post kit enforces this format.
- Covers are framed cards on velvet: blue for NVS, red for AXIOM, gold for FREQ.
- The location tag is always the reel's landmark.
- Keep the "AI content" label on.

## Rules (do every time)

1. **Hook in the first second.** The first frame must carry the line or the image that makes someone stop. The two breakouts opened on a strong, readable image. Never open on a slow fade from black.
2. **The first comment goes up within a minute of posting,** and it is a question from the kit. Reply to every comment within the first hour. Comments in that first hour are what keeps a reel being shown.
3. **Share every reel to your Story** with a poll or question sticker. Stories are 11% of views and almost no interactions, which is the cheapest thing to fix.
4. **Post reels as reels.** A cover posted as a photo (as happened with 028) gets photo reach, which is about 6% of views.
5. **Captions open with the hook, then a searchable phrase.** Instagram search reads captions, so the first line should be words people search for, e.g. "stoic motivation" or "self-mastery".
6. **Pin three posts:** the two breakouts (FRAMES, BEARING) and FREQ 001. New visitors judge the account by those.
7. **Test with Trial Reels.** Use them to try a new hook or cover on non-followers before it reaches your followers.

## Profile (convert viewers)

- **Bio:** say what NDCH is and when new work comes out. For example:
  `NDCH™ · visual philosophy`
  `Short stoic films, Tue & Fri`
  `FREQ: affirmations set to sound 🎧`
  `↓ start here`
- **Highlights:** keep ARTIFACTS and ORIGINS. Add **FREQ** and **START HERE** (three best reels, plus one line on what NDCH is).
- **Link:** go straight to the newest film or FREQ track, not only the shop.

## Measure weekly

For each reel, record:
- views and the share coming from non-followers
- average watch time and 3-second hold
- shares, saves and comments
- profile visits and follows

Targets for the next 30 days:
- At least 3 reels past 100 views.
- Profile visits at 5% of viewers.
- Comments on every post.
